## SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill

- **Process.** Establish one contribution and its evidence through brainstorming or by reading the existing record. Assign claims, displays and space to sections. The first manuscript prose is a disposable introduction, preceded by a sequence of topic sentences. In Story Mode, an identity paragraph comes before that introduction. Then draft Evaluation, Design, Background and Related Work, write the introduction afresh from the results, and write the abstract. Check the whole paper for coherence, then compress it. [SKILL.md](library/skills/SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill/SKILL.md:176) · [story_mode.md](library/skills/SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill/story_mode.md:111)
- **Rules.** Open paragraphs with claims, name mechanisms and metrics, justify design choices where they appear, and end each evaluation subsection with an interpretation. The default targets a mean of about 21 words per sentence and requires active voice. Define terms at first use, gloss them once, and qualify an empirical generalisation when it exceeds the evidence. [craft_reference.md](library/skills/SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill/author_profile/craft_reference.md:13) · [gate_semantic.md](library/skills/SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill/author_profile/gate_semantic.md:12)
- **Checks.** On every edit, run greps for passive constructions, negation, filler, punctuation and term drift. An independent reviewer reruns them and checks definitions, whether each passage follows from what precedes it, and whether claims and numbers have evidence. Repeat until major and critical findings are resolved. The judgement checks work on Markdown. Most greps can be retargeted from `sections/*.tex`, while submission checks assume LaTeX and PDF. [gate_mechanical.md](library/skills/SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill/author_profile/gate_mechanical.md:125) · [red_team_protocol.md](library/skills/SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill/red_team_protocol.md:8)
- **Evidence.** The design cites 7,600-plus Overleaf edits, 100-plus LaTeX versions and five peer-review cycles. Those histories document how a systems lab revised papers. They do not isolate the effect of the skill. A reported blind Story Mode test exposed gaps and prompted revisions, but the public examples are synthetic and the changelog gives no post-revision comparison. [DESIGN.md](library/skills/SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill/DESIGN.md:25) · [CHANGELOG.md](library/skills/SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill/CHANGELOG.md:22)
- **Borrow.** Use S1–S6 as questions for the reader’s editor, especially whether a term, quantity or pronoun is intelligible using only what the reader has seen so far. [gate_semantic.md](library/skills/SNL-UCSB_paper-writing-skill/author_profile/gate_semantic.md:12)
- **Avoid.** The bans on dashes and passives conflict with moderation. Requiring named mechanisms, numerical substitutes for adjectives and claim headings throughout would encourage three habits we are trying to reduce. Requiring repeated review before Barry sees the draft also reverses the agreed order.

## dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills

- **Process.** Gather and group the evidence, decide the main messages and sketch the paragraphs. Finish displays, then write the first manuscript prose in Results, followed by Methods, Introduction, Discussion and Abstract. Draft quickly, then read aloud, inspect verbs, label paragraphs by their purpose, cut, and ask an outside reader to explain the findings and why they matter. For a lay piece, start with the main finding and select details according to what the reader needs to understand it. [Process](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-process/SKILL.md) · [Manuscript](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-manuscript/SKILL.md) · [Outreach](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-outreach/SKILL.md)
- **Rules.** Reduce each sentence of a bloated paragraph to its point, merge duplicates, put the main point first and write the paragraph again. Repeat the same technical term across prose and displays. Report relationships in Results and leave most exact values in the table. Use past tense for completed actions and present tense for what a display shows. Keep a passive where the procedure matters more than the actor. [Paragraphs](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-prose/references/paragraphs.md) · [Sections](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-manuscript/references/sections.md)
- **Checks.** Four Python scripts accept Markdown. `extract_text.py` maps sections, `prose_audit.py` flags surface habits, `keyword_consistency.py` checks likely term drift and acronym definitions, and `numeric_consistency.py` flags competing sample sizes, abstract numbers absent from the body and suspect precision. A matching number anywhere in the body can satisfy the abstract check. The outside reader tests comprehension directly. [Scripts](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/README.md:75) · [Outside-reader pass](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-process/SKILL.md:117)
- **Evidence.** The Markdown fixture went from 165 to 112 words and from 25 to four flags in my rerun. The three old-versus-new skill comparisons both totalled 29 of 30 regex expectations. That tests detection more directly than reading quality. The outreach example itself changes the stated sample from 715,000 to 717,000. [Fixtures](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/examples/README.md) · [Comparisons](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/docs/eval-comparison.html) · [Lay examples](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-outreach/SKILL.md)
- **Borrow.** Take the outside-reader retelling, the paragraph rewrite from plain points, and the outreach instructions for deciding which details a short piece can omit. [Process](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-process/SKILL.md:117) · [Prose](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-prose/SKILL.md:89) · [Outreach](library/skills/dvdsosa_scientific-writing-skills/skills/sciwrite-outreach/SKILL.md)
- **Avoid.** Making finished figures a prerequisite can commit the writer to the order of the analysis. Mandatory acronym redefinitions add clutter. The outreach advice to make analogy, suspense and drama standard can displace literal explanation and concrete observation.

## HairuFan_research-writing-rhetorical-skill

- **Process.** Start with existing prose. Identify its section and study design, each sentence’s function, the relation between adjacent ideas and the evidence needed for each claim. Diagnose repetition and over-explanation. First write revised prose at step 7, then audit meaning, claim strength and naturalness. Revise again if a critical check fails. [SKILL.md](library/skills/HairuFan_research-writing-rhetorical-skill/skill/research-writing-rhetorical-skill/SKILL.md:44)
- **Rules.** Carry a shared term into the next sentence before reaching for a connector. Add a bridge sentence only if the relation still needs explaining. Preserve the comparison group, direction, magnitude, uncertainty and scope. Keep simulation claims within the conditions simulated, and distinguish an inconclusive estimate from evidence of no effect. [SKILL.md](library/skills/HairuFan_research-writing-rhetorical-skill/skill/research-writing-rhetorical-skill/SKILL.md:61) · [Meaning audit](library/skills/HairuFan_research-writing-rhetorical-skill/skill/research-writing-rhetorical-skill/references/meaning-preservation.md)
- **Checks.** Three model review passes and a benchmark rubric cover meaning, logical relations, claim calibration, naturalness and concision. A changed value, reversed comparison or invented result overrides the score. The passes can read Markdown directly. They require judgement and the relevant source context. [Evaluation rubric](library/skills/HairuFan_research-writing-rhetorical-skill/evals/rubric.md)
- **Evidence.** In 80 paired cases, the skill won 43, lost 21 and tied 16, with zero reported critical failures. Two blinded Gemini judge runs and selective human adjudication make this the strongest comparative evidence of the five. It covers short edits from one generator, Claude Sonnet 5. The 67% win rate among non-ties fell short of the stated 70% target. [Evaluation report](library/skills/HairuFan_research-writing-rhetorical-skill/evals/results/v1.0.0-evaluation-report.md)
- **Borrow.** Give the reader’s editor the naturalness audit and the writer the meaning audit for checking a compressed passage. Use the paired design if testing our own edits. [Naturalness audit](library/skills/HairuFan_research-writing-rhetorical-skill/skill/research-writing-rhetorical-skill/references/naturalness-audit.md) · [Meaning audit](library/skills/HairuFan_research-writing-rhetorical-skill/skill/research-writing-rhetorical-skill/references/meaning-preservation.md)
- **Avoid.** A sentence-by-sentence editing workflow inherits the old draft’s priorities. Preserving every qualification locally can perpetuate defensive prose. The evaluation also found revisions that inserted an unsupported contrast or narrowed a claim without evidence.

## FonaTech_Academic_Writing_Skills

- **Process.** Define question and audience, audit existing material, select literature, and record supporting and contrary evidence. Plan the question and evidence for each section, then first write prose in small sections at step 5. Develop displays alongside the text, translate if requested, build and preview, review independently, repair and rebuild, then seek author acceptance. Steps can overlap. [Workflow contract](library/skills/FonaTech_Academic_Writing_Skills/skills/sci-writing-orchestrator/references/workflow-contract.md)
- **Rules.** Start paragraphs with a concrete topic sentence and carry its subject or relation into the next sentence. Put the actor before a long modifier. Split at the second independent claim while keeping the condition and citation with the claim they govern. The English defaults are a median of 16–20 words and a ceiling around 30. Preserve distinctions such as measured versus modelled results. [Paragraph and sentence craft](library/skills/FonaTech_Academic_Writing_Skills/skills/sci-prose-style/references/paragraph-sentence-craft.md) · [Style rules](library/skills/FonaTech_Academic_Writing_Skills/skills/sci-prose-style/references/style-rules.md)
- **Checks.** `prose_lint.py` accepts Markdown and skips headings, tables and fenced code. It flags long or disconnected sentences, stacked hedges, repeated openers, undefined abbreviations and number formatting. It ran on the edited dvdsosa fixture but crashed on the original because the advisory entry matching “significant” lacks the `max` field the script expects. The separate numerical evidence audit requires structured project records or supported document inputs. [Prose linter](library/skills/FonaTech_Academic_Writing_Skills/skills/sci-prose-style/scripts/prose_lint.py:297) · [Numerical audit](library/skills/FonaTech_Academic_Writing_Skills/skills/sci-manuscript-review/scripts/audit_numbers.py)
- **Evidence.** The validation record reports 45 Python regression passes and a rendered bilingual synthetic manuscript. The 16 disciplinary cases are teaching scenarios. The demo establishes that the production workflow can build and check its own fixture. It used AI self-review and had no independent expert or human author assessment. [Validation](library/skills/FonaTech_Academic_Writing_Skills/VALIDATION.md)
- **Borrow.** Add a question and supporting and contrary evidence to each section in Barry’s existing plan. Use the advice on linking sentences to guard against choppy prose after shortening. [Writing plan](library/skills/FonaTech_Academic_Writing_Skills/skills/sci-writing-orchestrator/references/writing-plan-contract.md) · [Linked sentences](library/skills/FonaTech_Academic_Writing_Skills/skills/sci-prose-style/references/paragraph-sentence-craft.md:17)
- **Avoid.** Sentence-length targets and vocabulary caps can become editing objectives of their own. Keeping every condition next to every occurrence of a claim can restore the qualifications we cut. The build and translation machinery serves a different need from the writing guidance.

## K-Dense-AI_scientific-writing

- **Process.** Set up the workspace, choose reporting guidance, verify sources and register claims and results, then outline from that record. First write manuscript prose at step 5. Reconcile Methods and Results, verify citations, review authorship and declarations, check displays and reporting coverage, then lint and seek human approval. [SKILL.md](library/skills/K-Dense-AI_scientific-writing/SKILL.md:92)
- **Rules.** Bind each factual claim to verified evidence and give it one physical Markdown line with claim and evidence IDs. Describe Methods as performed, keep observation and interpretation distinct, and bound conclusions to the population and design. For binary trial outcomes, give counts, denominators and absolute and relative effects with uncertainty when required. These are mostly reporting rules. [Scientific fidelity and drafting](library/skills/K-Dense-AI_scientific-writing/SKILL.md:63)
- **Checks.** The file specifies a Markdown claim audit and linter, plus checks for numeric and method–result consistency, reference syntax and duplicates, authorship and reporting coverage. Claim auditing depends on the annotated Markdown and its registries. The supplied directory contains only `SKILL.md`, so I could inspect the specified checks but could not run them or verify their coverage. [Checks and listed files](library/skills/K-Dense-AI_scientific-writing/SKILL.md:177)
- **Evidence.** The file provides scaffold and command examples. The supplied material contains no prose comparison, benchmark result or executed test record.
- **Borrow.** Use the evidence outline’s distinction between verified support and unresolved issues. Barry’s existing ledger can hold both. [Evidence outline](library/skills/K-Dense-AI_scientific-writing/SKILL.md:147)
- **Avoid.** A second registry, a hash update after rephrasing and one claim per physical line would give evidence administration control over drafting. Use the reporting rules where the study or venue calls for them. Applying them wholesale to a two-page brief would crowd out its argument.

## Across the five

All five ask the agent to settle what the text should say before polishing sentences, then check that the wording preserves the evidence. Several already supply parts of our process. SNL checks what a first-time reader knows at each point. dvdsosa asks a reader to retell the argument and gives the brief fewer details. HairuFan tests whether editing changes meaning. FonaTech plans sections around evidence and alternatives. K-Dense specifies traceability.

None combines the agreed roles in order. One writer drafts both pieces afresh from the argument and evidence. Barry reads first for voice. A reader’s editor then reports where the argument becomes hard to follow. The agents who did the science check against the ledger and flag faults, leaving the wording to the writer. None tests whether that sequence prevents numbers and qualifications from returning as a good short outline becomes a full draft, or whether the result conveys a scientific view and gives readers something concrete to picture. [Founding note](docs/founding-note.md:40) · [Barry’s methods](docs/methods/README.md:22)

**None can replace our process.** dvdsosa is the closest starting point because it gives separate instructions for articles and lay summaries and checks what a reader understood. Keep Barry’s planning method and use the borrowed checks at the points where they help the writer or reader’s editor make a decision.
